How a deal earns its badge

  • NEW LOW The lowest price the source has ever tracked for this product.
  • STRONG DEAL A big, genuine cut — usually 30% or more off the usual price.
  • GOOD PRICE A solid saving, worth buying if you already wanted it.
  • ROUTINE SALE A small cut, or a price this product hits often. Not a reason to rush.

Method: each price was read on 27 Nov 2026 at the named store or in a deals roundup that names the store (9to5Toys, TechSpot, Mashable, Yahoo Shopping). The discount on every card is computed from the two prices shown, so the maths always adds up. Prices move fast on Black Friday — the store link on each card takes you to the product at that store to confirm before you buy.
